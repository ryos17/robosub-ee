                                       IV. C ONCLUSIONS
   We have presented a speech-to-text conversion-based diver
underwater communication system. Initial trial results show
promising outcomes of the proposed diver communication sys-
tem. Using a fine-tuned Whisper-base model, an average WER
of 2.4% is achieved during the trial with an average processing
time of 4.3 seconds. Future works include further improving
the reliability of the speech-to-text conversion algorithm and
the integration of the system to diver equipment.
