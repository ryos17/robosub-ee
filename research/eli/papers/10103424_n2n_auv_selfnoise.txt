                                                                                                                         Abstract—The passive detection performance of an
                                                                                                                     autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) is affected by various types
                                                                                                                     of noise. One of the main components of the noise is the AUV’s
                                                                                                                     self-noise. Inspired by the statistical reasoning shown in
                                                                                                                     Noise2Noise, we propose a deep learning model for AUV’s self-
                                                                                                                     noise suppression. It can learn the spectrum features of the noise
                                                                                                                     from single-channel data in a self-supervised learning setting and
                                                                                                                     reconstruct clean signals. The tests on South China Sea
                                                                                                                     experimental dataset show that the model is able to intelligently
                                                                                                                     suppress AUV’s self-noise for single hydrophone signals.

                                                                                                                        Keywords—autonomous underwater vehicle; Noise2Noise; noise
                                                                                                                     removal; self-noise
