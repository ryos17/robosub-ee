                                                                                                                                                                                         lenging task in reverberant settings such as the underwater

                                                                                                                                                                                         leveraged for subsequent, more accurate localization. Current
                                                                                                                                                                                         boundary estimation methods are constrained to a regime of
                                                                                                                                                                                         high signal strength, or mitigate noise with heuristic (subop-
                                                                                                                                                                                         timal) filters. These limitations can lead to fragile estimators
                                                                                                                                                                                         that fail under non-ideal conditions. Furthermore, many al-
