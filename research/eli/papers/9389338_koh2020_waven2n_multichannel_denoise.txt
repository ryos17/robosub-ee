C. Implementation with Hydrophone Array data
    Underwater acoustic signals are highly non-stationary and
suffer more attenuation than speech signals. This means that
underwater signals have a relatively larger dynamic range, and
suffer more frequency fading effects than speech signals. Hence,
it is important that spectral whitening and normalization by
standard deviation have to be first performed on the signal.

                                                                                     requiring any clean signal as the target. The model does not
                                                                                     need to be first initialized with weights from supervised
