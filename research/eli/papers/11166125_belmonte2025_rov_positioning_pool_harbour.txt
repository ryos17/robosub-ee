    Figure 2 shows the underwater acoustic detection system,
which combines hardware and software to capture, process,
and analyze underwater sounds. Hydrophones placed near
maritime traffic continuously record acoustic signals, which
are digitized and sent to Raspberry Pi 4b units. These devices
segment the audio into 3-second WAV files for analysis. Data
is transmitted efficiently via 5G or Ethernet, and results are
displayed through a user-friendly control panel.
