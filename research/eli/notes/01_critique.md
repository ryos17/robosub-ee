# Critique of the five sharpened directions (senior-author pass, 2026-09-06)

Ground truth used: `research/eli/README.md`, `notes/00_directions.md`, the paper extracts in `research/eli/papers/`, and a spot-check of the repo claims the skeptics leaned on. The spot-check confirms them: `gen2/daisyseed_firmware/master_ros.cpp:85` documents the wiring as "front = hydrophones 0 and 3, back = 1 and 2" (both intra-board pairs are parallel fore-aft baselines); `gen2/daisyseed_firmware/stream_audio.cpp:109` silently drops newest samples on host stall behind a u8 seq; `research/whisper_ivc/daisy_stream.py:109` says the cdc-acm path "loses AND reorders chunks"; the Teensy STABLE firmware has no IMU and the ESCs are Basic ESC (no RPM telemetry). The survey's "nobody" claims in `00_directions.md` sec 1.1, 1.2, 1.4 were IEEE-Xplore-coverage artefacts, not literature facts, and every direction paid for that.

Calendar reality: 13 weeks to the 7 Dec abstract, one working vehicle, three people, a sealed hull that must be opened for any clock fix, and gen3 power boards not yet ordered. That, more than novelty, decides the ranking.

## 2. Per-direction verdicts

**P1.** The novelty reviewer is right and the evidence holds: Ribeiro et al. ICME 2010 (5583886) already showed early reflections with a self-probed image-source model improve range and elevation for a compact array in air; DiffRIR (CVPR 2024) fits per-surface frequency-dependent reflection plus source directivity from ~12 RIRs and uses the fit as a simulator; Li & Chitre 10224658 and the Kari-Zhuang-Singer 2025 preprint put differentiable propagation models underwater; Misiurewicz 2022 (Sensors 22:1162) does 4-hydrophone multipath-aware TDOA in a 25 m pool; Yangzhou 2019 (JASA) benchmarks a DNN localizer against MFP in a reflective tank. Localization A is matched-field processing with a learned replica, and should be called that. What no one has done is the conjunction: vehicle-mounted sub-30 cm array, model fit from broadband CIRs in a real pool, the same fitted model used as MFP replica and as training simulator, and the fitted/generic/free-field ablation measured on real data on an embedded GPU. The feasibility reviewer is also right: the floor-mounted receiver makes the bottom image coincident with the direct path (13-40 us, under the 60 us resolution cell), the surface-echo slope is 10-18 us/m at 15 m, so range must come from side-wall images that Chitre 2023 Sec. IV warns are hypersensitive to wall irregularities; an order-2 shoebox cannot represent a 3 s reverberant tail, so a full-length STFT loss is dominated by unmodelled reverb; and a 1e5-point grid x 8192 bins x 125 images is 0.4 T complex MACs per frame, not real time. The GT budget (+-10-15 cm horizontal in practice, not +-5) is the same size as the 10-30 cm 3D error the proposal wanted to headline. What survives, and is still the best paper on the table: an early-reflection self-calibrated channel model (direct + surface + 4 first-order side walls, optionally 2nd-order vertical; learnable hydrophone body-frame positions, per-channel gain/FIR, per-wall SVF reflection filter per Lee 9837411, c, wall offsets) fit on a windowed early CIR (first 10-20 ms) with an envelope-then-phase loss; a coarse-to-fine delay-domain MAP localizer on the Orin with latency/power; and the central ablation of the same small net trained on free-field (Song 11104616 recipe), generic shoebox, and the fitted model, evaluated on the same real poses. Headline metrics: azimuth MAE and range error in 0.5 m bins vs GCC-PHAT bearing + single-hydrophone surface-echo range (Li 7485369). Elevation is reported as a stated limitation, not a result.

**P0.** Both skeptics hold. Woodward, Reiss & Magnasco 2020 (PLOS ONE, figshare 7956212) is a public 16-hydrophone, 14-surveyed-position, ML-localization dataset in a 33.5 m concrete aquarium pool; Lu 2024 (IEEE DataPort 10.21227/2mh4-4r49) is a public tank array-DOA dataset with angle labels; Srivastava et al. 2023 (JASA) and Neural-SRP already did the per-ingredient ISM realism ablation in air; 11616801 (Yan 2026) and 10891008 (Zhang 2025) already did sim-pretrain/real-finetune underwater. "First public pool hydrophone dataset" and "sim2real gap study" are both dead as headlines. The feasibility objections are decisive too: elevation spans a few degrees in a 2 m deep pool and the surface image lands inside the chirp resolution cell, so elevation labels would be noise; 8 azimuths at 45 deg spacing cannot separate CNN interpolation failure from sim2real gap (Song 2025 reports exactly that blow-up outside trained azimuths); and the "SAI slave over three wires" fix does not exist on Daisy Seed (only SAI2 is on the header). The narrowed claim survives and is worth a dataset paper plus Student Poster: first public recordings from a compact 4-element array mounted on an AUV hull in a bounded reverberant pool, azimuth + coarse-range labels, thruster on/off, bare-frame control subset, cross-board clock residual documented as a spec, with the gap attribution restricted to the vehicle-borne ingredients (hull manifold, thruster self-noise, clock error) that Woodward/Lu/Srivastava have no analogue for. It falls out of P1's sessions at zero extra pool cost and is the insurance abstract.

**D4.** Refuted on both axes and the evidence holds. 10103424 (Underwater Technology 2023) is self-supervised N2N AUV self-noise suppression from the vehicle's own recordings; Wang et al. JASA 2026 (Speed-UT2-CGAN) feeds vehicle speed as a conditioning input trained on real self-noise mixtures; Gulli et al. EURASIP 2025 is per-rotor-speed conditioning of lightweight mask networks with ablations, in air; Zhao et al. JASA 2026 targets propeller shaft harmonics; the Ocean Eng. 2023 piUSBL ANC paper already shows reference-free ANC restoring bearing. The survey line "no self-noise cancellation conditioned on thruster telemetry" was wrong. On the hardware: there is no IMU on the Teensy, Basic ESCs give no RPM, telemetry is ad-hoc `Serial.println`, and audio blocks have no host timestamp, so the conditioning vector the method needs does not exist and the DDSP RPM-locked branch reduces to audio-driven f0 tracking. Sixty minutes of clamped bollard thrust is also exactly what the gen2 power boards overheated on. The surviving piece is one subsection of P1: ~10 thruster-on poses at fixed PWM (<=50-60%, short bursts), a PWM-indexed Wiener/spectral-subtraction table as the suppressor, and a robustness curve of the localizer with and without it. If the table matches a learned model on steady thrust (physics says it will), that is a sentence, not a paper.

**P2.** Refuted; the evidence is strong. UWLoRa+ (10623112, Xie 2024) did LoRa-style FSCM in an indoor swimming pool with preamble-detected per-path combining in the dechirp domain, which is the proposed bin-profile matched filter written as a sum of shifted windows; VTRM CSS receivers (9990102 and the EURASIP 2021 paper) are the older form. AquaHelper (SenSys 2023) is consumer-speaker chirp + multiple pool hydrophones + wall-reflection-aware direct-path selection + TDOA position with no TX-RX clock; AquaApp/Gollakota (SIGCOMM 2022/2023) own "consumer device as underwater transmitter"; 9706010 and 10618907 own "CSS packet doubles as localization signal". Feasibility adds: at SF8-9/BW 4 kHz with a ~30 ms pool tail and dominant direct path, Steinmetz 9905674 shows plain dechirp already syncs 100% and lands at low-single-digit SER, so the decoder has little to improve; a 90 s per-pose file cannot hold 6 frames at each of four SFs (one 32-symbol SF9 frame is 5.4 s), so the symbol budget is ~800 not 2000; the Lloyd's-mirror overlap at 4 kHz bandwidth kills elevation and range-from-arrival claims; and the cross-board sync mod is unbuilt. What survives is cheap and real: one CSS frame type appended to the P1 probe file (zero extra pool time), offline plain-dechirp vs bin-profile SER vs pose class, intra-board preamble azimuth, and a one-paragraph Whisper-over-audio WER vs text-over-CSS CER comparison hooking Rong 11104740. That is a section of P1 or a second poster, not a paper, and it slides to Dec-Feb as `00_directions.md` already schedules.

**D6.** Novelty is technically intact (no published hydrophone-cued mechanically-scanned sonar) but the concept is textbook: passive-cued active track initiation (6290467 FUSION 2012, 6263410), cued passive bearing in distributed fusion (4632243), the AMP hydrophone-triggered acquisition package (Polagye 2020, jmse8080553), and robot-audition active audition. The headline "order of magnitude" is a scan-fraction identity that Hansen 10609976's timings already let a reviewer compute, and at pool ranges a full sweep is ~6-12 s, not 25-35. Feasibility is where it dies for this cycle: the repo's own wiring (`master_ros.cpp:85`) makes both intra-board pairs parallel fore-aft baselines, so the "orthogonal diagonals, no cross-board sync" trick requires re-cabling two hydrophones through the hull of the only vehicle; the 8-bearing x 10 m polar grid does not fit in a ~12.5 m wide pool; a GoPro on a ladder covers ~6 x 4 m of 20 m of trajectory; the Ping360 stepper couples into the audio band; and there is zero Ping360/ping-python code in the repo, on a USB hub documented as marginal. The surviving claim (time-to-first-contact and wall-echo false-contact rate with a passive HPD gate, static raised vehicle near an end wall) is a poster at best and competes for the same three people and the same hull-opening window as P1's clock fix. Drop it for OCEANS 2027; it becomes attractive in 2028 once P1 has delivered a calibrated array and a Ping360 driver exists.

## 3. Recommended paper shape

**Title.** Self-calibrated early-reflection channel model for beacon localization with a hull-mounted 4-hydrophone array in a reverberant pool, running on the vehicle GPU

**Abstract (3 sentences).** Compact hydrophone arrays on small AUVs cannot recover range from time-differences alone, and in bounded environments such as pools, tanks and harbour structures the wall echoes that corrupt bearing estimates also carry that missing range information. We fit a differentiable early-reflection image-source model of a 25 m concrete pool (learnable per-wall reflection filters, hydrophone body-frame positions, source response, sound speed and cross-board clock residual) to broadband chirp recordings from a hull-mounted 4-hydrophone array at ~30 surveyed source poses, and use the fitted model as the replica for a coarse-to-fine matched-field localizer that runs in real time on the vehicle's Jetson AGX Orin. On held-out poses the fitted-model localizer improves range error over GCC-PHAT bearing plus single-hydrophone surface-echo ranging while matching its azimuth accuracy, and the same fitted model as a training simulator closes a measured fraction of the sim-to-real gap for a small learned localizer relative to free-field and generic-shoebox simulation, with latency and power reported for every estimator.

**Contributions.**
- A data-fitted differentiable early-reflection model (Lee 9837411 SVF filters + Chitre 10224658 geometric error terms) for a hull-mounted array in a bounded volume, fit on windowed early CIRs with an envelope-then-phase coarse-to-fine loss; positioned explicitly as the underwater, vehicle-mounted counterpart of Ribeiro 2010 and DiffRIR, and as the first real-array pool test of the Chitre/Singer differentiable-propagation recipe.
- Joint self-calibration of hydrophone body-frame positions, per-channel hull-scattering FIRs and the inter-board clock residual from known-pose data, with the residual reported as a measured quantity (this is the one piece nobody has had to solve because nobody else recorded through two free-running ADC boards).
- A coarse-to-fine, delay-domain matched-field localizer in plain torch ops on the Orin, with latency, throughput and power at MAXN and 15 W, benchmarked against GCC-PHAT LS bearing, SRP-PHAT on the same grid, incoherent MUSIC, Li 7485369 surface-echo range and Kasetkasem 8096292 OMP first-arrival bearing.
- The central ablation: one small learned localizer trained on (a) free-field simulation (Song 11104616 recipe), (b) generic shoebox, (c) the fitted model, evaluated on the same real held-out poses, with and without N-pose fine-tuning. This number has not been reported for hydrophone arrays.
- A thruster-on robustness subsection (PWM-indexed Wiener suppression, ~10 poses) and, if time allows, one CSS packet type in the probe file giving intra-board preamble bearing and a Whisper-vs-text paragraph (Rong 11104740).
- Public release of the raw 4-channel 96 kHz recordings with pose labels, hull geometry, water temperature and clock residual (the P0 dataset), submitted separately to the Student Poster Competition.

**Experiments.**
1. Bench, weeks 1-2: inter-board drift and USB start-offset measurement (electrical click into both boards, 10 min); speaker characterization in a bucket (WAV vs codec, 1-20 kHz response, THD at three levels, directivity, DAC ppm from chirp time-scaling); drop-free recorder validated at 2x96 kHz/24-bit for 30 min with zero seq gaps; renderer sanity check against pyroomacoustics at c=1480.
2. Pool session 1 (fit set, ~3 h, thrusters off): sub on the flat deep-end floor at a taped mark aligned to a lane line (tether power), speaker clamped facing down on a rigid pole with depth stop and bubble level, taut marked line along a lane rope for along-pool distance, water temperature logged; 25-30 poses at 1-12 m, two depths, 2 s chirp spacing (pool RT60 ~3 s), then ~8 thruster-on poses at two PWM levels. Record a 1 m speaker-response clip first.
3. Fit + MAP localizer, weeks 8-10, laptop then Orin; report log-spectral distance and predicted-vs-measured early-echo delays on held-out poses.
4. Pool session 2 (eval set, ~3 h): 25-30 new poses including a held-out range band and a second sub position rotated 90 deg (transfer test); one slow continuous walk for tracking; same thruster-on subset.
5. Ablation training runs (free-field / generic shoebox / fitted) and Orin latency/power, weeks 10-12; abstract by 7 Dec on session 1 + fit + MAP; session 2 feeds the full paper.
6. Metrics: azimuth MAE and 90th percentile; range error in 0.5 m bins by range band (0-5, 5-10, >10 m); 3D error reported only against a stated GT budget of +-10 cm / +-3 deg; miss rate; per-estimator ms/frame and W.

**Single biggest risk and mitigation.** The clock and capture chain. Without sample-synchronous 4-channel capture the array collapses to two parallel intra-board pairs with a left/right ambiguity and no 3D anything, and every downstream claim goes with it; and the current firmware drops samples silently. Mitigation, decided by end of week 2 on measured drift: primary path is a class-compliant 4-input USB audio interface at 96 kHz fed from the existing hydrophone preamps (one ALSA device, one clock, drop detection for free, ~$200, no firmware), integrated during the single hull-open window; parallel fallback is master GPIO 1 Hz pulse into slave EXTI with a 32-bit sample counter stamped into every USB frame and host-side piecewise-linear resampling, plus a fixed ring-overflow marker in `stream_audio.cpp`. If neither is proven on the bench with an electrical click by end of week 3, the paper scope drops to azimuth-only from intra-board pairs and the range contribution moves to the surface/wall-echo single-channel path; the abstract is written to survive that.
